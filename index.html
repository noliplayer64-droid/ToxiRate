ToxiRate

An educational venom risk mapping and safety tool. To be used if you have just had an encounter with a dangerous animal followed by a venomous bite or sting. Also to be used if you are generally aware of animals you may encounter.

Definitions

  • HLT: (Human Lethality Threshold) Estimated number of envenomations required to be fatal in an average healthy adult. How many stings or bites are required to kill you. To find measurements for the HLT, simply search "how many (your animal) bites/stings are lethal to humans".
  • COW: (Critical Onset Window) Minutes before likely fatality. To find measurements for the COW, simply search "how long does a fatal ammount of (your animal) venom take to kill".
  • VRR: (Venom Risk Rating) Combined danger score (and system works completely differently to LD50) from 1 (low) to 10 (extreme).The scale works completely differently to LD50.
  • DISCLAIMERS: If you have any allergies to wasps and other animals, Apologies, this app is based on stats for the average human. The HLT is based on a 25-year old person in good health.
  • PLEASE REMEMBER: If you are attacked by an animal you deem dangerous, look it up before coming here. you may be mistaken of what it is, as many milksnakes look like the extremely venomous coral snake. Do your research!

VRR Scale

VRRMeaning
0–1–2Low risk, refer to HLT for whether to see a doctor.
3–4Moderate risk, wise head to a hospital, treatment may be required
5-6 Severe risk, head to a hospital, treatment highly suggested
7-8 Highly Severe risk, get to a hospital swiftly, treatment urgently required
9–10Extremely lethal, head to hospital immediately. Immediate treatment required. Call an ambulance swiftly.
