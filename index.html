Definitions

HLT = Human Lethality Threshold, COW = Critical Onset Window (minutes), VRR/PRR = Venom/Poison Risk Rating (0–10).

  • HLT: Estimated number of envenomations required to be fatal in an average healthy adult.
  • COW: Minutes before likely fatality after envenomation — shorter windows need urgent care.
  • VRR/PRR: Combined danger score (0–10) derived from HLT and COW. Lower HLT and shorter COW raise the score.
